Our story

A dream come true for our family, and now for yours.

Kyleu Bush Camp is named after our two sons, Kyran and Leuan. It's a family-run camp that's very close to our hearts, and we promise that every guest who stays at Kyleu feels comfortable.

We set out to offer affordable, luxurious bushveld camping for tents, rooftop tents, off-road trailers and caravans, a memorable, unique bush experience for all ages and abilities. Choose a secluded spot deep in the bush, or stay inside the fence near the pool for a more family-orientated weekend.

Good to know

Questions campers ask us

Where exactly is Kyleu Bush Camp?

Kyleu is inside the Dinokeng Game Reserve, about 50 km north of Pretoria (Tshwane) and roughly two hours from Johannesburg. Take the N1 north to the Hammanskraal off-ramp, enter at the Dinokeng Tau Gate and follow Kaallaagte Road. Full directions & map.

Is the camp fenced? Is it safe with children?

We have both. The Fenced Bush Camp (7 stands) is fenced off, ideal for families with young children. The Open Bush Camp (10 stands) and Self-Sufficient Sites (6 stands) are unfenced, so the Big 5 can walk through camp. Camping is at your own risk and children must be supervised at all times.

Is there electricity?

No, there's no electricity at any of the stands. Ablutions have solar LED lights and gas geysers for hot water. Bring your own lighting, battery packs or solar.

How much does it cost to camp at Kyleu?

Ablution stands are R160 per person per night on weekdays and R245 on weekends (min. 2 nights). Self-sufficient stands are R100 weekdays and R180 weekends. Children under 12 are free, and pensioners get a weekday special. Dinokeng's own conservation fees are paid separately to the reserve. See all rates.

Can I bring a caravan, off-road trailer or rooftop tent?

Absolutely. Stands fit anything from a single rooftop tent to 4-5 trailers or caravans. Each stand's page tells you how many setups it can take.

Are pets allowed?

Sorry, no pets are allowed. Kyleu is in a Big 5 reserve.

How do bookings and payments work?

Choose your stand and dates online and send your booking with your details. A 50% deposit secures your booking, with the balance due 7 days before arrival (bookings within 7 days are paid in full). Payment & cancellation policy.
