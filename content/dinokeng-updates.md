## Keeping the balance: why live game capture matters

date: 2026-10-30
image: assets/img/dinokeng/bush-telegraph-live-game-capture.jpg

Why does Dinokeng move some animals to other reserves? The veld can only feed so many animals, and when there are too many it gets overgrazed. The latest Bush Telegraph explains how live game capture keeps the reserve healthy for every animal that lives here.

## A fresh start for the bush: block burns in the south
type: Bush Telegraph
date: 2026-10-22
image: assets/img/dinokeng/bush-telegraph-block-burns.jpg
file: assets/docs/dinokeng/bush-telegraph-block-burns-2026-10-22.pdf
size: 0.8 MB

After the first rains, Dinokeng burns about 4,000 hectares of veld in the south of the reserve, one block at a time. It clears old, dry grass so fresh grazing can grow, and the animals love the new green shoots. You may see smoke or burnt patches on your drive in: this is planned and carefully managed.
