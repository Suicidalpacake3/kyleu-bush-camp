# Kyleu Bush Camp: Stands

> **To add a new stand:** copy any block below (from `## Name` down to the line before the next `##`), paste it at the bottom, and change the details.
> **To add or change photos:** put the photos in the folder `assets/img/stands/<folder name>/` and list the file names after "Photos:", separated by commas. The first photo is the cover photo.
> **To hide a stand for a while:** set "Status:" to `closed`. Use `new` to show a "New stand" badge.
>
> - Area must be one of: Fenced, Unfenced, Self-sufficient (this decides the price and the icons shown).
> - Map position = where the stand sits on the camp map, as "left %, top %" (0, 0 is the top-left corner).
>   Easy way: open the Stay & Book page with `?mapedit` at the end of the address (e.g. stay.html?mapedit#map),
>   click the spot on the map and the position is copied for you to paste here. Leave empty to leave the stand off the map.
> - Highlights are separated by commas.
