## Summer is here — the pool is open!
date: 2026-10-24
image: assets/img/photos/pool-lapa.jpg
summary: Hot Dinokeng days call for a cool-down. Our pool in the fenced camp is open for all Kyleu guests — fenced and unfenced.
tags: Camp news, Family

Dinokeng summers are beautiful, but they can get *hot*. Good news: the Kyleu pool in the fenced camp is sparkling and open for all our guests — whether you're staying inside the fence, on an unfenced stand or out on the spruit.

### Good to know
- The pool is in the fenced camp, next to the lapa.
- Children must be supervised at all times.
- Please shower before swimming — sunscreen and bush dust don't mix with pool water.
- Glass is not allowed at the pool.

Grab ice from the camp shop (R15 a bag) on your way in and make a lazy afternoon of it. See you at the water!

## New stand on the spruit: Self-Sufficient 6
date: 2026-08-02
image: assets/img/wildlife/tree-sunset.jpg
summary: Meet our newest stand — a private, completely self-sufficient spot on the Kaallaagte spruit.
tags: New stand, Self-sufficient

We've opened a sixth self-sufficient stand along the Kaallaagte spruit. Like its neighbours it has **no facilities** — no water, no electricity and no braai — which is exactly why our self-sufficient campers love it.

### What to bring
- All your water (plan for at least 10–15 litres per person per day)
- Your own power — solar, a battery pack or a generator-free setup
- A braai stand or fire basket (fires only in the loose boma provided)
- A porta potty, or hire one from us for R100 a night (including cleaning)

[Book Self-Sufficient 6](stay.html?area=self-sufficient)

## What to pack for your first Kyleu weekend
date: 2026-06-14
image: assets/img/photos/sunset-rooftop-tent.jpg
summary: No electricity, wild animals and the best sunsets in Gauteng. Here's our checklist for a smooth first visit.
tags: Tips, First-timers

Kyleu is wild camping — there is **no electricity** on the stands, and on the open and spruit stands there's no fence between you and the Big 5. Here's what our regulars never leave home without.

### The essentials
- Extra lighting (the ablutions have solar LED lights, but we can't promise sunshine every day)
- A battery pack or solar panel to keep phones and fridges going
- A gazebo for shade during the day
- Cooler boxes — ice is available at the camp shop
- Closed shoes and a torch for walking around at night

### Remember
- Never feed the animals and pack food away at night.
- Fires only in the braai areas provided — no firewood collecting from the veld.
- No music, and quiet time from 22:00.
- Dinokeng's conservation and self-drive fees are paid separately to the reserve — you can pay them online before you arrive.

See you in the bush!
