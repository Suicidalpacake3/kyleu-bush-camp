Book with confidence

50% deposit secures your stand. Balance due 7 days before you arrive. Booking 12 days or less before you arrive? Then you pay in full.

Children under 12 free

Only adults and pensioners are charged. Bring the children along and make memories in the bush.

Weekend = Fri, Sat & Sun

Friday, Saturday and Sunday nights are weekend nights, and so is the night before a public holiday. A stay with a weekend night is at least 2 nights: Friday and Saturday, Saturday and Sunday, or Sunday and Monday.

Anything from the camp shop? (optional)

Add firewood, ice and more now and it goes on this quotation, so you pay for everything at once. It is waiting at reception when you arrive. You can still add more later with the link to your booking.

Have a credit from a cancelled booking? (optional)

Type the credit code from your cancellation email and it comes off this booking. It works with the email address of the booking that was cancelled.

Once your booking is paid in full, we email you a link to finalise it: your vehicles and who is in each one, and roughly when you arrive.
