Inside 1-7

Fenced Bush Camp

Seven spacious stands in a fenced-off area, each surrounded by bushveld and many positioned right near the fence for animal viewing. Stroll through camp at your leisure and get in touch with nature.

Each stand has a large private ablution with a toilet, a shower overlooking the bushveld, a basin and a small wash-up area, with hot water and solar LED lights. And Kyleu boasts a pool in the fenced area to cool down on a warm summer's day.

See fenced stands
Stands 1-10

Open (unfenced) Bush Camp

Ten rather exclusive campsites with no fence between you and the wild, the Big 5 and other animals are free to roam. What better way to experience the bushveld?

You still get your own private ablution with hot water, a braai and a free bag of wood every night. Guests in the open camp are welcome to use the pool in the fenced area.

Please note: camping in the open bush camp is entirely at your own risk. Treat all animals with respect. They are wild.

See open stands
Self-Sufficient 1-6

Self-Sufficient Sites

For the avid bush camper: six spacious stands relatively far apart along the Kaallaagte spruit, a beautiful setting with lots of trees and greenery that the animals just love.

There's no ablution (no water, no electricity) and no braai, just a small loose boma on the ground for your fire. You need to be completely self-sufficient. Porta potty hire is available.

Dinokeng fees
Dinokeng fees

Paid to the reserve, not to us

Dinokeng Game Reserve charges a conservation fee per person per night (capped per stay) and a once-off self-drive permit per vehicle. These are settled separately from your Kyleu booking.

  • Buy online before you go, or at the gate
  • Overnight conservation fees are capped at R300 per person per stay
  • You may only drive the main roads unless you have an official self-drive route permit
  • Speed limit in camp and the reserve is 30 km/h

Explore Dinokeng

A Big 5 reserve on Pretoria's doorstep

Dinokeng is a 21 000 ha conservation area made up of more than 170 private properties whose owners dropped their fences to let wildlife roam, the only free-roaming Big 5 reserve in Gauteng.

Self-drive game viewing

Around 140 km of tracks to explore. Expect wildebeest, impala, warthog, zebra and, with luck, lion, elephant and rhino.

The open east

The eastern section is open, savannah-like country, great for giraffe, red hartebeest and big herds of zebra.

Veldjierondom picnic spot

A kiosk, braai facilities and a viewing platform with a fantastic panorama over the bush.

Craft beer stop

Finish a drive at Arlington Brewery for a local craft beer or a slushy, with a restaurant on site.

Wilderness Way route

Off the main roads you need an official self-drive route permit, buy online, at the gates or at the route entrance.

Night sounds

With no electricity at the stands, the stars are spectacular. Listen out for hyena, jackal and lions calling.

What to pack & camp rules

Pack this

  • Extra lighting: solar lights can't promise sunshine every day
  • Battery pack or solar panel (no electricity at the stands)
  • A gazebo for shade during the day
  • Cooler box: ice is sold at the shop
  • Torch and closed shoes for walking at night
  • Self-sufficient stands: all your water, a porta potty (or hire one) and a fire basket

Please don't

  • Bring pets (none are allowed)
  • Play music, and keep noise to zero after 22:00
  • Make fires outside the designated braai areas
  • Collect firewood or kindling from the bushveld
  • Feed or approach animals, or touch snakes. Stay still and report sightings
  • Litter. Please use the bins provided
Can I bring day visitors?

Yes, day visitors are welcome as your guests at an additional charge, by prior arrangement with management. They must leave camp by 18:00.

How far apart are the stands?

Stands are 40-80 m from each other, so you get real privacy and a tranquil bushveld experience.

How many people fit on a stand?

Most stands take 6-8 guests; our larger stands (Inside 4, Inside 6, Stand 2, Stand 6 and Self-Sufficient 2) take 10-12 guests and 4-5 setups. Contact us for larger groups.

Is Kyleu good for families?

Very. Children under 12 camp free, there's a pool, and the fenced camp gives extra peace of mind. Children must be supervised at all times.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Written cancellations within 14 days of arrival are charged 50% (the deposit), and within 48 hours 100%, unless the booking can be moved to another suitable date. Full policy.
