Terms & policies

KYLEU Bush Camp is situated in the Dinokeng Big 5 Game Reserve. Dangerous animals walk around freely. Please take the time to read our terms and conditions — it's your responsibility to know the rules of the camp to ensure a pleasant stay.

Visitor's responsibility

Camping at KYLEU is ENTIRELY AT YOUR OWN RISK. KYLEU Bush Camp and its owners will not be responsible for any injury, loss or damage to persons or personal property while on the premises or on excursions in the greater Dinokeng.
  • Insurance of personal items
  • Supervise children and minors staying with you
  • Respect the privacy of other guests
  • Report any irregular deeds by fellow guests to KYLEU management
  • Use the available facilities responsibly and with accountability

Camp areas

Open / unfenced bush camp

Our open bush camp consists of 10 rather exclusive campsites. Each stand is spacious and surrounded by bushveld where the Big 5 and other animals are free to roam. This area is not fenced. Each stand has a private ablution — no electricity.

Fenced bush camp

Our fenced bush camp consists of 7 exclusive campsites. Each stand is spacious, positioned near the fence for animal viewing, and surrounded by bushveld. Each stand has a private ablution — no electricity.

  • Stands allow for roughly 6–12 guests per night depending on the stand — see each stand's details.
  • Stands are 40–80 m from each other for a tranquil bushveld experience.
  • Each ablution stand has its own private ablution with a shower, toilet and hand basin, plus a wash-up for dishes, with hot water.
  • Ablutions have solar-powered LED lights — we cannot ensure sunshine every day, so please bring additional lighting.
  • There is NO ELECTRICITY at the campsites.
  • Each ablution stand has braai facilities and 1 complimentary bag of wood per day's stay. Extra wood is available for purchase.
  • Fires only in the designated area — NO OPEN FIRES. No firewood or kindling may be collected from the bushveld.
  • Do not attempt to touch or play with snakes. Stay calm and still until the snake moves away, and report sightings to management.
  • All animals should be treated with respect — they are wild.

Self-sufficient sites

We have 6 relatively exclusive, UNFENCED campsites on the spruit with NO FACILITIES — an extremely tranquil area the animals just love. There is no ablution (no water, no electricity) and no braai facilities. You need to be completely self-sufficient. Porta potty hire is available.

General terms & conditions

  • All prices quoted are subject to change without prior notice.
  • Weekends and long weekends can only be booked as a whole.
  • Prices for KYLEU do not include the Dinokeng levy, which is payable to the reserve.
  • No pets are allowed on the property.
  • Smokers should always take care when extinguishing a cigarette butt.
  • Fires are ONLY to be made in the designated fire pit areas, and must be completely extinguished before leaving the area or going to bed.
  • Day visitors are allowed (as your guests) at an additional charge by prior arrangement with management, and must vacate the camp by 18:00 at the latest.
  • NO MUSIC allowed. Zero noise after 22:00.
  • Make sure your tents, caravans and trailers are locked when leaving the premises.
  • Please use water sparingly.
  • Unless otherwise indicated, the speed limit within the camp and the reserve is 30 km/h.
  • Guests may only drive on the main thoroughfare or servitude roads within the reserve unless in possession of an OFFICIAL SELF-DRIVE ROUTE PERMIT (available online, at the reserve entrance gates or at the entrance to the Wilderness Way route).
  • KYLEU has a no-littering policy — please use the rubbish bins provided.
  • Should any rules not be adhered to, we reserve the right of admission. Misbehaviour will not be tolerated, and guests failing to adhere to the rules will be asked to vacate the premises immediately, with no refunds.

Payment policy

  • Deposit: 50% of your reservation must be paid within 5 days of receiving your invoice to secure your booking. If not received, your booking is automatically cancelled.
  • Balance: the balance is due 7 days before your arrival date.
  • If the reservation is made 7 days or less before arrival, the full amount must be paid immediately to secure the booking.
  • Please send all proofs of payment to info@kyleu.co.za.

Accepted methods: EFT / bank transfer, deposit and cash. Online card payments are shown in this website mock-up as a planned feature.

Cancellation policy

  • Written cancellation within 14 days before the arrival date is charged at 50% (i.e. the full deposit) if the booking cannot be moved to another suitable date.
  • Cancellation within 48 hours before the arrival date is charged at 100% if the booking cannot be moved to another suitable date.

Amendments

  • No credit will be given for any decrease in the number of people on your booking.
  • If the number of guests increases, any additional charges must be paid in full before arrival.

In case of an emergency please contact management on 060 458 4478 or info@kyleu.co.za. KYLEU management thanks you in advance for respecting the rules — we hope you have an enjoyable stay!

Privacy (POPIA)

We only collect the personal information we need to manage your booking and stay — your name, contact details, vehicle details and booking information. We use it to confirm and invoice your booking, to prepare the gate and guest list, to contact you about your stay and, only if you opt in, to send occasional news and specials.

We never sell your information. We share it only where needed to deliver your stay (for example our booking and payment providers) or where the law requires it. Card details are processed by a PCI-DSS compliant payment provider and are never stored by Kyleu.

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing info@kyleu.co.za. Our Information Officer is Sherri Symons.

PAIA manual

We are committed to transparency and the protection of personal information. Our PAIA Manual, prepared in accordance with the Promotion of Access to Information Act, 2000 (PAIA), explains what records we keep and how to request access to them.
