Directions from Pretoria

Turn by turn

  1. Travel north on the N1 from Pretoria
  2. Take the Hammanskraal off-rampThrough the toll gate
  3. Turn RIGHT and go over the highway
  4. Take the first street LEFT
  5. Continue to Dinokeng Tau GateTake a ticket at the gate
  6. Travel 4.8 km along the main roadSpeed limit 30 km/h, watch for animals
  7. Turn RIGHT onto Kaallaagte dirt roadContinue for 2 km
  8. Turn RIGHT into Kyleu Bush CampLook for our sign, and the occasional cheetah 🐆
