About Dinokeng

A Big 5 reserve on Pretoria's doorstep

Dinokeng is a 21 000 ha conservation area about 50 km north of Pretoria. It is made up of more than 170 private properties whose owners dropped their fences so wildlife can roam freely.

It is the only free-roaming Big 5 reserve in Gauteng and one of very few in the world inside the borders of a major metro. Day visitors and overnight guests share around 140 km of reserve roads.

Reserve fees

Paid to the reserve, not to us

Dinokeng Game Reserve charges a conservation fee per person per night (capped per stay) and a once-off self-drive route permit per vehicle. These are paid to the reserve, separately from your Kyleu booking.

Your Kyleu partner code. Kyleu is a Dinokeng Certified Partner, so our guests pay the reserve's lower partner rate. We email you a voucher code in the week of your stay: use it when you pay online, or show it at the gate.

  • Fees can be paid online before you go, or at the Dinokeng gate
  • Overnight conservation fees are capped at R340 per person per stay
  • RSA pensioner rates (Monday to Thursday) are only available at the gate
  • You may only drive the main roads unless you have an official self-drive route permit
  • Speed limit in camp and the reserve is 30 km/h

Things to see and do in Dinokeng

Guided drives and walks, self-drive routes, picnic spots and places to stop for a meal or a cold one.

Everything on Dinokeng's website

Self-drive game viewing

Around 140 km of tracks to explore. Expect wildebeest, impala, warthog, zebra and, with luck, lion, elephant and rhino.

The open east

The eastern section is open, savannah-like country, great for giraffe, red hartebeest and big herds of zebra.

Guided drives and bush walks

Qualified field guides offer game drives and bush walks in the reserve. Walks take 3 to 4 hours in the sun, need a fair level of fitness and aren't suited to children. Ask us, or book through Dinokeng.

Picnic spots and a bird hide

Bayethe, Dula Nokeng and Veldjierondom have braai facilities, toilets and water (Bayethe's water isn't for drinking). Birders will enjoy the MarSu Lombert bird hide.

Places to eat and drink

Stop at Arlington Brewery & Cidery, the Rusty Rhino Bushveld Market & Pub, Kwalata or Mongena, among others. Trading hours differ and many ask you to book ahead.

The Self-Drive Route

More than 140 km of tracks, most of them fine for a sedan. You need a self-drive route permit on top of your conservation fees: buy it online or at the gate.

Night sounds

With no electricity at the stands, the stars are spectacular. Listen out for hyena, jackal and lions calling.
